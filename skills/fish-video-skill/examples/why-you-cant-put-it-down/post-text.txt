TITLE
Why You Can't Put Your Phone Down

DESCRIPTION
You don't check your phone because it's good. You check because you never know what's next.
The psychology of random rewards, from lab experiments to slot machines to your feed.

- Unpredictable rewards keep you checking
- Slot machines pay out the same way
- The feed feels like the same loop
- Take away the surprise
