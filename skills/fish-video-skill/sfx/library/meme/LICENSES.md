# Licences of the meme sound pack

Twelve short recordings, trimmed, faded and peak-normalised from the originals named below, then encoded as Ogg Vorbis. Each licence was re-checked on 2026-11-05 against the file's page (Wikimedia Commons, and the Freesound or SoundBible page where the file came from there); the hash of every original matches the file Commons serves.

- **CC0 and public domain**: no credit is needed.
- **CC BY 3.0 / 4.0**: the author must be credited when a video plays the sound. `fishvideo render` adds the credit to the project's `assets/credits.json` for every pack sound a video actually uses, and `fishvideo package` prints it. Changes made here: trimmed, faded, level-normalised, re-encoded.
- These are not the famous viral sounds, and none of them is copyrighted material taken from a film, a show or a game. Left out on purpose after checking: a drum roll (a CC0 label on a file taken from YouTube), a cash register (no licence on its page), breaking glass (permission rests on a forum post), a buzzer (licence not checkable), the Wilhelm scream (CC0 label doubtful) and an audience boo (marked both CC BY-SA and "public domain, made by an algorithm").
