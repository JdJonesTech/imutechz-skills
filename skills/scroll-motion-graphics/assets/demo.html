Scroll motion
graphics.

A canvas film whose only clock is your scroll position. Five reels, each a pure function of progress, so it seeks both ways like a video. Captions are measured every frame and the drawing never runs under them. Then the same timeline becomes a control: a story drum you can scroll, drag, click or step with the arrow keys.

  • Canvas 2D
  • Zero dependencies
  • Pure functions of p
  • Never pins input
  • Reduced motion
  • 360 → 1920

The contract

Six rules this page keeps.

  • Scroll is the clock.p in [0, 1] comes from the pinned track. Nothing animates on a timer.
  • Reels are pure.draw(ctx, t, env) has no state and no randomness, so it scrubs both ways.
  • Words live in the DOM.Captions are real text, measured every frame; the canvas lays out around them and is clipped as a safety net.
  • Never pin input.No wheel, touch or key capture. Controls seek by scrolling the page.
  • Idle costs nothing.The loop stops when p settles and when the section leaves the viewport.
  • Reduced motion.No smoothing, no in-between drum roll, no caption animation. The content is all still there.
